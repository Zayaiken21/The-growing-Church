How this website handles your information.

Browsing and accounts

Public pages do not require an account. If you register, your email and authentication details are processed by the church’s Supabase project. Your connection preference and login session may be stored in this browser.

Recovery and contact

A pastor can issue a temporary recovery PIN for a specific account after verifying the member’s identity. Email contact links open your email app. Giving and sermon links may lead to external services with their own privacy policies.

External resources

Fonts may load from Google Fonts, the authentication client from jsDelivr, and custom images or videos from sources configured by church administrators.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact the church about your account or personal data.
