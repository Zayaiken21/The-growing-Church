A few things you might wonder

What should I wear?

Wear what feels comfortable. You are welcome as you are.

Can I bring my children?

Families are welcome. Ask the church team about age groups, supervision, and check-in before your visit.

Do I need to know the Bible?

No. Bring your questions, and take part at your own pace.

How can I ask for prayer?

Contact our church team directly. Share only what you feel comfortable sharing.
