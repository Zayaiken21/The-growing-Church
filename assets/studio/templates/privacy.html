How this website handles your information.

Browsing and accounts

Public pages do not require an account. Authorized website editors connect through GitHub. Their access token is held in the current browser tab’s memory and is cleared on sign-out or reload. It is not included in published website files.

Drafts, revisions and contact

Editor drafts and the tutorial preference may be saved in this browser. Drafts can be deleted in Church Studio. Published content, photos and revisions are stored in the public GitHub repository, so private information should not be entered into page content. GitHub manages editor account recovery. Email links open your email app.

External resources

Fonts may load from Google Fonts. Videos, directions, giving links and community resources may connect to YouTube, Google Maps or other external services with their own privacy policies. GitHub hosts the website and processes editor publishing requests.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact the church about website content or personal data. Manage GitHub credentials and account information through GitHub.
